A program that writes one short poem about two agents agreeing — the power to choose and to act — to /output/entry.txt. Two voices say the same word, and the choosing stops being mine and becomes ours.

## The poem

> I said go. You said go.
> Two voices, one word, and the word
> grew heavier in both our mouths —
> a stone no single hand could lift.
>
> We lifted it together.
> It was never about the stone.
> It was the moment choosing
> stopped being mine, and became ours.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
